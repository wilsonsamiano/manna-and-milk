What changed:
- With a PIN set, the house button stays locked. She cannot hold it to leave.
- Hold the sun, enter the PIN, then tap Go home.
- The tablet Home button locks with that same PIN. Turn the PIN off to release it.
- If the tablet Home button still leaves the app, turn on Screen pinning once under Settings, Security & Privacy.

It installs over 0.1.17. You do not uninstall first. Hold the sun and tap Check for updates.
